🏏 What is CricScore?

CricScore is an AI-assisted cricket scoring platform that makes it easy to run matches, manage tournaments, and share live scorecards — all from your phone.

Whether you're organising a local T10 gully tournament or a full multi-team season, CricScore handles ball-by-ball scoring, team rosters, fixtures, standings, and detailed player stats automatically.

👥 Who is it for?

Scorers — tap through each delivery quickly. Wickets, extras, boundaries, and wides all tracked with one or two taps.

Tournament organisers — create tournaments, add fixtures, invite co-scorers by email, and publish standings in real time.

Players & fans — follow live scorecards, review match summaries, and browse full player statistics.
