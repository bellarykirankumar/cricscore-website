Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🏏 About CricScore

CricScore helps users score and manage cricket matches, tournaments, teams, fixtures, and scorecards. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

📦 Information We Collect

  • Account credentials — your email address and password used to create and access your account.
  • Cricket data — match, tournament, team, and player information that you enter into the app.
  • Device permissions — camera, microphone, and photo library access may be requested for video clip and highlight features.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

  • User authentication and account security
  • Storing and displaying your cricket scoring data
  • Enabling live scoring, scorecards, and highlight clips
  • Improving app features and performance

🔒 Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely using AWS cloud infrastructure. Authentication is handled through secure identity services with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

📷 Device Permissions

Camera, microphone, and photo library permissions are only activated when you actively use features that require them — such as recording video clips of match highlights. You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
